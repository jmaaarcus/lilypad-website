3. Free version, Lilypad Pro and billing

3.1 Free version

The free version of Lilypad includes up to 3 notebooks and 1 whiteboard, all writing tools, 2D shapes, PDF import and annotation, Find, search inside PDFs, automatic outlines, presentation mode and export. Notebooks in the Trash do not count toward these limits. Split View, audio recording, and graphs, geometry and 3D shapes require Lilypad Pro.

3.2 Lilypad Pro plans

Lilypad Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, available monthly, quarterly (every 3 months) or yearly. The price of each plan is shown in the app before you buy. Lilypad Pro removes the free version's limits and unlocks every Pro feature.

3.3 Payment and renewal

Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm your purchase. Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each period at the current price, unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the period ends. Apple charges your account for the renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.

You can cancel at any time. On your device, open the Settings app, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Lilypad Pro, then tap Cancel Subscription. After you cancel, Lilypad Pro stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. Deleting your Lilypad account does not cancel your subscription, so cancel it first if you no longer want to be charged.
