Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lilypad handles personal information when you use the Lilypad app for iPad and the Lilypad website. Lilypad is made by Anh Quoc Vo, an individual developer based in Vietnam ("we", "us" or "our").

Lilypad is designed so that your notebooks stay with you. They are stored on your iPad and in your own private iCloud account, and we cannot see them.

Summary

  • Your notebooks, pages, handwriting, PDFs, images and recordings are never sent to us. They stay on your device and in your own iCloud.
  • To run your account, we store your Apple user identifier, the name and email address you choose to share through Sign in with Apple, and sign-in session records.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your card details.
  • Lilypad has no advertising, no analytics tools and no third-party tracking. We never sell your personal information or use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in Settings → Account → Delete Account.

1. Who We Are

The data controller responsible for your personal information is Anh Quoc Vo, Vietnam. You can contact us about anything in this policy at quocvo1707@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

Lilypad requires you to sign in with Apple. When you do, we receive and store:

  • Your Apple user identifier, a random ID that Apple assigns for Lilypad. It is not your Apple ID password, and we cannot use it to access your Apple account.
  • Your name and email address, if you choose to share them. If you use Hide My Email, we receive only Apple's private relay address.
  • Records of your sign-in sessions, and the dates your account was created and last used.
  • A token from Apple, stored encrypted, which we use only to revoke Sign in with Apple when you delete your account.

2.2 Your Notebooks

Notebooks, pages, handwriting, typed text, PDFs, images, audio recordings, outlines and settings are stored on your device and, when iCloud is turned on for your Apple ID, synced by Apple through your private iCloud database. We have no access to this content. Search, text recognition, outline generation and PDF processing run entirely on your iPad.

2.3 Purchases

Lilypad Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment, and the app checks your subscription status on your device with Apple's StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card or billing details.

2.4 Device Permissions

Lilypad asks for your permission before it uses the following, and uses them only when you use the related feature:

  • Microphone, to record audio notes.
  • Camera, to take a photo to insert into a page.
  • Photo library, to insert images you select.

Recordings and images are saved only in your notebooks, on your device and in your iCloud. You can change these permissions at any time in the iPad Settings app.

2.5 Crash Reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPad settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us. These reports do not identify you personally.

2.6 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your notebook content, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing history. The Lilypad website does not use cookies or analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Create and operate your account, and sign you in on your devices.
  • Keep each account's notebooks separate, so that they are never merged between Apple IDs.
  • Protect Lilypad against abuse and keep the service secure.
  • Delete your account and revoke Sign in with Apple when you ask us to.
  • Respond to your questions and support requests.
  • Comply with our legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, for profiling, or for automated decisions that affect you.

3.1 Legal Bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on the following legal bases: the performance of our contract with you, to provide your account and Lilypad Pro; our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and working properly; compliance with legal obligations; and your consent where we ask for it, such as for device permissions.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers. Our account server runs on Cloudflare Workers, and account records are stored in MongoDB Atlas. These providers process data on our behalf, only to provide their services to us. When the app contacts our server, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver and protect the request; we do not store IP addresses in your account record.
  • Apple. Sign in with Apple, iCloud and the App Store are provided by Apple and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us.
  • Change of ownership. If Lilypad is sold or transferred, account information may be transferred to the new owner, who must continue to protect it as this policy describes.

5. International Transfers

We are based in Vietnam, and our service providers may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the standard contractual clauses offered by our providers.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • Account information is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Sign-in sessions expire automatically after 60 days and are then deleted.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account record and sessions from our server and revoke Sign in with Apple. Your notebooks are deleted from your device and, if iCloud sync is on, from your iCloud at the same time.
  • If you stop using Sign in with Apple for Lilypad, or delete your Apple ID, Apple notifies us and we delete your account record and sessions from our server.
  • We may keep limited information for longer when the law requires it.

7. Security

Account data is sent over encrypted connections. Sign-in tokens are stored hashed or encrypted, and access to our database is restricted. On your iPad, your sign-in details are kept in the iOS Keychain. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you of a breach where the law requires it.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You can:

  • Delete your account and its data at any time in Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  • Export your notebooks as PDF or Lilypad Notebook (.llpd) files before you delete anything.
  • Sign out at any time, or stop using Sign in with Apple for Lilypad in your Apple ID settings.
  • Turn device permissions on or off in the iPad Settings app.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights, email quocvo1707@gmail.com. We will respond within the time the law requires, and we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

8.1 EEA, UK and Switzerland

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8.2 California

California residents have the right to know, delete and correct their personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information as California law defines those terms, and we do not use sensitive personal information for any purpose that would require an opt-out.

9. Children

Lilypad is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
